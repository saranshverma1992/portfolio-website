● Design Decisions

Where I Chose a Harder Path

Three moments where the obvious solution wasn't the right one — and what changed my mind.

Option Considered Why I Rejected It What I Chose Instead
One-click, fully automated document generation Business docs carry project-specific nuance AI can miss — auto-publishing risked pushing inaccurate requirements into development. AI drafts first, humans review/edit/approve every section before publishing.
Separate tools per document type (BRD, Use Cases, Test Cases, etc.) Users re-entered the same project info repeatedly; context fragmented across tools; navigation broke down. One centralized project workspace — single source of truth, reusable context.
Collect all project details upfront before AI starts Long onboarding, higher abandonment — users often didn't know every detail on day one. Capture essentials first, progressively enrich context as the project develops.
KEY INSIGHT
"The fastest-looking option was rarely the one users could trust. Every 'no' here bought back user confidence."

● Thankyou

What This Project Taught Me

Reflecting on B2B SaaS workflows, design systems, and the implementation of user-controlled artificial intelligence.

01

Design Systems Scale

Establishing robust tokens and layout principles early on enabled the AI generator to output highly consistent documentation layouts effortlessly.

02

AI Needs Human Trust

Users adopted Specio AI far faster when they retained complete control to review, edit, discard, or approve every generation stage.

03

Think Beyond Screens

The most impactful UX optimizations came from redesigning the end-to-end documentation lifecycle rather than refining isolated page mockups.

04

Business Context Matters

Deeply mapping the collaboration thresholds between BAs, QA, and devs was essential to frame a platform that all roles could comfortably share.

"The most valuable design decision wasn't adding AI — it was designing a workflow people could trust."

This project strengthened my approach to designing AI-powered enterprise products by combining product strategy, systems thinking, and human-centered design into one cohesive experience.
